# 60-Panel Environs Comic — Notes

## Working title

**Computation and Learning Environs: Kitech, Robot EDU, WalkWise / SQM+**

## Purpose

This comic explains the three primary UMA learning environs through who, what, where, and why:

1. Kitech / Kitchen Lab — Measured World.
2. Robot EDU — Measured Machine.
3. WalkWise / SQM+ — Measured Self.

## Format

- 6 pages.
- 10 panels per page.
- 60 panels total.
- Professional cheerful storyboard/comic style.
- Intended for archive review, public explanation, and possible teacher-guide front matter.

## Page sequence

| Page | Focus | Primary question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kitech: The Measured World at Home | What is Kitech? |
| 2 | Kitech: Who, Where, and Why | Who learns here and why does it matter? |
| 3 | Robot EDU: Measured Machine in Motion | How does measurement become motion/control? |
| 4 | Robot EDU: Who, Where, and Why | Who learns with robots and where does it happen? |
| 5 | WalkWise SQM+: Measured Self in Motion | How does measurement turn toward human motion? |
| 6 | WalkWise SQM+: Who, Where, and Why | Who is supported and why does quality matter? |

## Recurring character

A friendly older gray-haired educator-engineer with glasses serves as guide, witness, teacher, and archive keeper.

## Tone

- Informative.
- Cheerful.
- Professional.
- Human.
- Non-hype.
- Supportive, especially for WalkWise / SQM+.

## Key message

```text
Measured World · Measured Machine · Measured Self — computation and learning begin where life, motion, and care become measurable.
```
