# WalkWise / SQM+ — One-Pager

**Pillar:** Measured Self

## One-sentence purpose

WalkWise / SQM+ is a supportive human-motion learning environ where wearable inertial measurement helps observe, compare, summarize, and encourage motion quality.

## Who it serves

- Older adults who want to preserve mobility and confidence.
- Families and helpers who want clearer, kinder motion information.
- Educators connecting physics, sensors, computation, and human movement.
- Developers building supportive, non-diagnostic wearable tools.

## Where it happens

A hallway, living room, park path, neighborhood sidewalk, classroom corridor, or any safe walking space where motion can be observed.

The lab for Measured Self is wherever the body moves.

## What it measures

| Motion idea | Possible signal source | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Cadence | step timing | How often steps occur. |
| Rhythm | timing regularity | Whether motion has a steady pattern. |
| Symmetry | left/right pattern comparison | Whether the motion is balanced. |
| Consistency | repeatability across time | Whether the pattern holds together. |
| Stability | sway/lurch/bob indicators | Whether motion appears controlled and steady. |

## Language policy

WalkWise / SQM+ must remain supportive and non-diagnostic.

Use:

```text
Observe → Compare → Summarize → Encourage
```

Avoid:

- diagnosis,
- prescription,
- commands,
- medical claims,
- pretending the device can decide what a person should do.

## Why it matters

Measured Self closes the UMA loop. Measurement is not only for objects and machines. It can also help a person understand motion, confidence, rhythm, and daily function in a careful, humane way.
