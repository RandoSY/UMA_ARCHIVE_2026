# Glossary

## UMA

Universal Motion Assessment / broader umbrella for the user's measurement-centered educational and human-motion architecture.

## Measured World

The pillar focused on measuring physical reality: mass, temperature, volume, time, energy, distance, and observable phenomena.

## Measured Machine

The pillar focused on machines, robots, controllers, sensors, feedback, and motion under control.

## Measured Self

The pillar focused on supportive measurement of human motion, rhythm, consistency, and agency.

## Kitech

Kitchen Lab / Kitchen Technology: the home kitchen as a serious measurement and computation learning environment.

## Nugget

A low-cost embedded learning pathway from simple timing and small PIC/AVR systems into machine literacy.

## REDBoard Revival

The AVR/Arduino/Nano shield-world teaching platform centered on visible, low-cost, repairable instrumentation.

## AVR Spirit

The educational ethos of low-level, 8-bit, close-to-the-metal learning through AVR/Arduino/Nano systems.

## PIC Black Box

A small PIC microcontroller used as an inspectable programmable black box for measurement and control.

## Programmable 555

The idea that a PIC can be taught as a programmable timing/logic device analogous to a 555 timer.

## One-Bit Lab

A lab family where one-bit events or states become meaningful data through sampling and statistics.

## Yellow Pad Test

Before adding a metric or instrument, define and estimate it manually on paper so the physical meaning is clear.

## Mathematical Soul

The central mathematical revelation inside an experiment, such as damping in the Singing Ruler.

## Singing Ruler

A clamped ruler/cantilever instrumented with an IMU to reveal oscillation, damping, FFT, and ODE concepts.

## Smart eScale

A load-cell/HX711-based scale used for KitchenLab mass, calibration, and uncertainty experiments.

## Tapeless Ruler

A distance-measuring instrument using ToF or ultrasonic sensing.

## Robot EDU

The robot education pillar where measurement becomes machine motion, control, and feedback.

## Prospector Robot

An exploration/mapping/reporting robot concept rooted in a responsible undersea-mining narrative.

## ECHONAV

Robot orienteering/navigation based on sensing, echo, heading, and confidence.

## WalkWise

A supportive mobility measurement environment focused on observing and encouraging useful motion.

## SQM+

A wearable motion-quality summary meter concept for supportive, non-diagnostic feedback.

## Naggler

A friendly reminder/encouragement companion in the Measured Self family.

## Tone Trainer

A resistance-training/motion-rhythm support tool in the UMA/MER family.

## Tai Chi Timer

A slow rhythm/flow/timing support activity for deliberate movement practice.

## WebBLE

Browser-based Bluetooth Low Energy communication, commonly through Chrome.

## WebSerial

Browser-based serial communication for USB-connected devices.

## BLE NUS

Nordic UART Service, used as a BLE text-channel pattern.

## UMA Text Protocol

A shared plain-text command/data message protocol for UMA classroom instruments.
